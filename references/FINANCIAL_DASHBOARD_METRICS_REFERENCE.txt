================================================================================
FINANCIAL DASHBOARD METRICS REFERENCE
Sections: Income Statement Highlights | Capital & Liquidity | Key Ratios
================================================================================

TIER GUIDE
Tier 1 = Core / primary dashboard metric
Tier 2 = Important supporting metric
Tier 3 = Contextual / deeper-dive metric

ANALYST NOTES
• For absolute dollar metrics, there is no universal "good" dollar value because company size varies. Their VALUE RANGE therefore focuses on profitability, trend, and growth.
• For percentage ratios, ranges are broad investor-oriented benchmarks and should always be interpreted with industry, business model, and company maturity in mind.
• Operating Income Growth, Net Income Growth, and EPS Growth should not be interpreted normally when the current or prior comparison period is negative or near zero.
• Backlog is most useful for contract-driven, aerospace, defense, infrastructure, industrial, and project-based businesses.


================================================================================
1. INCOME STATEMENT HIGHLIGHTS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 1 — CORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Revenue
DEFINITION: Total sales recognized during the reporting period.
IMPACT: Shows the size of the company's top line and how much business it generated before expenses.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Very High / Core)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is generally better when growth is sustainable and profitable.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0% YoY growth: Contracting
  • 0%-10%: Slow growth
  • 10%-20%: Healthy growth
  • 20%-40%: High growth
  • > 40%: Hyper-growth

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Gross Profit
DEFINITION: Revenue - cost of revenue.
IMPACT: Shows how much money remains after directly delivering the product or service.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Very High / Core)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better, especially when gross profit grows faster than revenue.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0: Gross loss
  • Around 0: Gross break-even
  • > 0: Positive gross profit
  • Best trend: Positive and growing faster than revenue

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Operating Income
DEFINITION: Gross profit - operating expenses.
IMPACT: Shows whether the core business is profitable after normal operating costs but before interest and taxes.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Very High / Core)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better; positive and expanding is preferred.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0: Operating loss
  • Around 0: Operating break-even
  • > 0: Operating profit
  • Best trend: Margin expansion and sustained positive growth

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Net Income
DEFINITION: Profit remaining after operating expenses, interest, taxes, and other income or expenses.
IMPACT: Shows the company's final GAAP profit attributable to the reporting period.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Very High / Core)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better; consistent positive growth is preferred.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0: Net loss
  • Around 0: Net break-even
  • > 0: Net profit
  • Best trend: Positive and consistently growing

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Free Cash Flow
DEFINITION: Operating cash flow - capital expenditures.
IMPACT: Shows how much cash remains after funding the capital investment required to operate and grow the business.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Very High / Core Cash Generation)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0: Negative free cash flow
  • Around 0: FCF break-even
  • > 0: Positive free cash flow
  • Strongest signal: Positive FCF with expanding FCF margin

--------------------------------------------------
METRIC: Operating Cash Flow
DEFINITION: Cash generated from the company's normal business operations.
IMPACT: Shows whether the company's core business is converting activity into real cash.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Very High / Core Cash Generation)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better; positive and consistently growing is preferred.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0: Negative operating cash flow
  • Around 0: Cash break-even
  • > 0: Positive operating cash flow
  • Best trend: Positive and growing faster than revenue
--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Capital Expenditures
DEFINITION: Cash spent on property, equipment, infrastructure, and other long-lived operating assets.
IMPACT: Shows how much cash must be reinvested into the physical or technical asset base.
SCORE: Tier 1 (High / Required for FCF)
VALUE MEASURE: Lower relative to operating cash flow or revenue is generally better, unless investment is driving attractive growth.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 5% of revenue: Low capital intensity
  • 5%-10%: Moderate
  • 10%-20%: High
  • > 20%: Very High

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Diluted EPS
DEFINITION: Net income available to common shareholders / diluted weighted-average shares outstanding.
IMPACT: Shows the amount of GAAP profit attributable to each diluted share.
SCORE: Tier 1 (High / Core Per-Share Profitability)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < $0: Loss per share
  • Around $0: Per-share break-even
  • > $0: Positive EPS
  • Best trend: Positive EPS with sustainable YoY growth


================================================================================
2. CAPITAL & LIQUIDITY
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 1 — CORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Cash & Cash Equivalents
DEFINITION: Cash and highly liquid short-term investments readily available to the company.
IMPACT: Shows the company's immediate liquidity and financial flexibility.
SCORE: Tier 1 (High / Core Liquidity)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is generally safer, but excessive idle cash can reduce capital efficiency.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 3 months of operating expenses: Thin liquidity
  • 3-6 months: Adequate
  • 6-12 months: Strong
  • > 12 months: Very Strong
  • Best evaluated together with debt and cash burn

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Total Assets
DEFINITION: Total economic resources controlled by the company.
IMPACT: Shows the size of the company's asset base and provides context for capital intensity and balance-sheet scale.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Balance Sheet Context)
VALUE MEASURE: Context-dependent; asset productivity matters more than absolute size.
VALUE RANGE:
  • Declining: Asset base shrinking
  • Stable: Little change
  • Growing: Asset base expanding
  • Strongest signal: Assets growing with healthy revenue and returns

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Total Liabilities
DEFINITION: Total obligations owed to creditors, suppliers, employees, governments, and other parties.
IMPACT: Shows the amount of claims against the company's assets and the overall liability burden.
SCORE: Tier 3 (Balance Sheet Risk Context)
VALUE MEASURE: Lower relative to assets, equity, and cash flow is generally safer.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 40% of total assets: Low
  • 40%-60%: Moderate
  • 60%-80%: High
  • > 80%: Very High
--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Backlog / Remaining Deal Value
DEFINITION: Contracted or committed future revenue not yet recognized.
IMPACT: Shows visibility into future business already signed or committed.
SCORE: Tier 1 (High for Contract-Driven / Project-Based Companies)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is generally better when backlog quality and conversion are strong.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0.5x annual revenue: Low visibility
  • 0.5x-1.0x: Moderate
  • 1.0x-2.0x: Strong
  • > 2.0x: Very Strong
  • Evaluate backlog growth, duration, and cancellation risk


================================================================================
3. KEY RATIOS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 1 — CORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Gross Margin
DEFINITION: Gross profit / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows how much of each revenue dollar remains after direct delivery costs.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Very High / Core Profitability)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 20%: Low
  • 20%–40%: Moderate
  • 40%–60%: Strong
  • 60%–80%: Very Strong
  • > 80%: Exceptional / Common mainly in high-margin software models
  • Industry context is required

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Operating Margin
DEFINITION: Operating income / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows how much operating profit is generated from each dollar of revenue after core operating expenses.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Very High / Core Profitability)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0%: Operating loss
  • 0%–10%: Thin
  • 10%–20%: Healthy
  • 20%–30%: Strong
  • > 30%: Excellent

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Net Margin
DEFINITION: Net income / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows how much final GAAP profit remains from each dollar of revenue after all expenses.
SCORE: Tier 1 (Very High / Core Profitability)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0%: Net loss
  • 0%–5%: Thin
  • 5%–10%: Moderate
  • 10%–20%: Strong
  • > 20%: Excellent

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: SBC / Revenue
DEFINITION: Total stock-based compensation expense / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows how much of revenue is being consumed by equity compensation and highlights potential dilution and earnings-quality concerns.
SCORE: Tier 1 (High / Core for SBC-Heavy Growth Companies)
VALUE MEASURE: Lower is better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 5%: Low / Healthy
  • 5%–10%: Moderate
  • 10%–20%: High
  • > 20%: Very High

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 2 — IMPORTANT SUPPORTING RATIOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Operating Expense / Revenue
DEFINITION: Total operating expenses / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows how much of each revenue dollar is consumed by sales, R&D, and corporate overhead.
SCORE: Tier 2 (High / Cost Structure)
VALUE MEASURE: Lower is generally better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 40%: Lean
  • 40%–70%: Moderate
  • 70%–100%: High
  • > 100%: Operating expenses exceed revenue

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Sales & Marketing / Revenue
DEFINITION: Sales and marketing expense / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows sales-acquisition intensity and how much revenue is being reinvested into growth.
SCORE: Tier 2 (Growth Efficiency)
VALUE MEASURE: Lower is generally better if revenue growth remains strong.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 10%: Low
  • 10%–25%: Moderate
  • 25%–40%: High
  • > 40%: Very High

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: R&D / Revenue
DEFINITION: Research and development expense / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows how much revenue is being reinvested into product and technology development.
SCORE: Tier 2 (Innovation Intensity)
VALUE MEASURE: Context-dependent; sustained investment should support future growth or competitive advantage.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 5%: Low
  • 5%–15%: Moderate
  • 15%–25%: High
  • > 25%: Very High

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Income Tax Expense / Benefit
DEFINITION: Current-period provision for income taxes or tax benefit recognized in earnings.
IMPACT: Shows the tax effect between pre-tax income and net income.
SCORE: Tier 3 (Tax Context)
VALUE MEASURE: Context-dependent.
VALUE RANGE:
  • Tax benefit: Often associated with losses or discrete tax items
  • 0%-15% effective rate: Low
  • 15%-30%: Typical broad range
  • > 30%: High
  • Not meaningful when pre-tax income is negative or distorted

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 3 — CONTEXTUAL / CONDITIONAL RATIOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

METRIC: G&A / Revenue
DEFINITION: General and administrative expense / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows the corporate overhead burden relative to the size of the business.
SCORE: Tier 3 (Cost Discipline)
VALUE MEASURE: Lower is generally better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 10%: Efficient
  • 10%–20%: Moderate
  • 20%–30%: High
  • > 30%: Very High

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Cost of Revenue / Revenue
DEFINITION: Cost of revenue / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows the portion of sales consumed by direct delivery costs and is the inverse of gross-margin economics.
SCORE: Tier 3 (Supporting Unit Economics)
VALUE MEASURE: Lower is better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 20%: Very Low
  • 20%–40%: Moderate
  • 40%–60%: High
  • > 60%: Very High
  • Equivalent interpretation should remain consistent with Gross Margin


--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Largest Segment Revenue Mix
DEFINITION: Revenue from the company’s largest segment or customer category / total revenue.
IMPACT: Shows how dependent the company is on one major business segment or customer type.
SCORE: Tier 3 (Concentration Context)
VALUE MEASURE: Lower concentration is generally safer, although strategic specialization can be intentional.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 60%: Relatively Balanced
  • 60%–80%: Concentrated
  • > 80%: Highly Concentrated
  • Analyze together with segment growth and customer concentration

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Gross Margin Excluding SBC
DEFINITION: (Gross profit + stock-based compensation included in cost of revenue) / revenue.
IMPACT: Shows gross margin before stock compensation embedded in direct delivery costs.
SCORE: Tier 3 (Non-GAAP / SBC Context)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better, but GAAP gross margin remains the primary metric.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 20%: Low
  • 20%–40%: Moderate
  • 40%–60%: Strong
  • 60%–80%: Very Strong
  • > 80%: Exceptional
  • Compare the gap versus GAAP gross margin

--------------------------------------------------

METRIC: Contribution Margin
DEFINITION: Company-defined contribution profit / revenue; the exact calculation can vary by company.
IMPACT: Shows the profitability of revenue after selected direct and customer-acquisition costs and can help evaluate scaling efficiency.
SCORE: Tier 3 (Company-Specific Non-GAAP)
VALUE MEASURE: Higher is better.
VALUE RANGE:
  • < 0%: Negative contribution
  • 0%–20%: Low
  • 20%–40%: Moderate
  • 40%–60%: Strong
  • > 60%: Very Strong
  • Compare only when the company’s definition is understood and consistent
